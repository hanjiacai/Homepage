Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at Southwest University in Chongqing, China, where I began my doctoral studies in September 2026.

Previously, I pursued my master's studies at the College of Computer and Information Sciences, Chongqing Normal University, from September 2022 to June 2025. From September 2025 to June 2026, I worked as a full-time faculty member at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Chongqing Youth Vocational & Technical College, Chongqing, China.

My research interests include online learning, incomplete data streams, learning with missing features, and concept drift.
